# Pharmacy data check

Checked: 2026-10-23. Source: each pharmacy's own website or store page, except latitude/longitude for non-chain pharmacies, which come from the U.S. Census Geocoder. "not found" means the field was not on the pharmacy's public pages.

Times are 24-hour clock. Counter hours do not include the lunch break; the break is listed separately.

**Nearby clinics** are within about 1 mile in a straight line from the pharmacy's coordinates (not walking distance). Sources: CVS MinuteClinic locator (only one Boston MinuteClinic found: Charlestown), the two urgent care centers in Boston proper on the Mass General Brigham and AFC location pages, and the HRSA public list of federally funded community health center sites (the data behind findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov). Left out: shelter-based sites for people experiencing homelessness, WIC offices, PACE/elder programs, dental-only sites, and pharmacies. Health centers that are not federally funded (for example some hospital-run neighborhood clinics) are not in the HRSA list, so a "none found" result can miss them. Health center walk-in policy was not on the list, so it says "not found (call first)".
